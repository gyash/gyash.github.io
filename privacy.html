FinanceOS privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

The short version

Your financial data stays on your iPhone. FinanceOS has no server, no account and no analytics, and it never sells or shares your data.

What FinanceOS reads, and why

  • Bank SMS you send to the app through an iOS Shortcuts automation you set up: to record payments and money received.
  • Gmail (read-only), if you sign in with Google: emails from banks, card issuers, fund houses and travel companies are read to record payments, read statements, confirm SIPs and group trips. Other emails are not opened. FinanceOS cannot send, change or delete email.
  • iCloud Mail, if you add it: the same, using an app-specific password stored in the iPhone Keychain.
  • Statement PDFs you share with the app or that arrive by email: to check and complete your records. Passwords you type for them stay in the Keychain.
  • Contacts, only the people you pick when splitting a payment: their name and phone number, to address a WhatsApp message you choose to send.

Where your data goes

  • Stored on your iPhone, in the app's own storage, and in backups only where you choose to save them (for example a folder in your iCloud Drive).
  • Google user data obtained through the Gmail API is used only to provide these features inside the app on your device. It is not transferred to anyone, not used for advertising, and not read by any person. FinanceOS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Ask (optional): questions are answered on the iPhone by Apple's on-device model. Only if you add your own Anthropic API key does FinanceOS send your question and a summary of the relevant figures to Anthropic to answer it, under your own Anthropic account.
  • Public fund prices are downloaded from AMFI; no personal data is sent to get them.
  • WhatsApp opens only when you tap to send a message, with the text shown to you first.

Your control

You can sign out of Gmail or remove iCloud Mail in the app's settings at any time, and revoke FinanceOS's Google access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on your iPhone.
