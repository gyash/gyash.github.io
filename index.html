FinanceOS

A personal finance app for iPhone, by Yaswanth Goteti.

FinanceOS reads your bank alerts (SMS and email) and bank statements to show what you spent, what's due, what you own and owe, and the people you've lent to. Everything is worked out on your iPhone. There is no FinanceOS server and no account.
